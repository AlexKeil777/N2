N2 Team · Vaporised hydrogen peroxide decontamination §1 Overview

Decontaminate up to 10× faster than legacy VHP systems.

We are the N2 Team, vaporised hydrogen peroxide decontamination specialists with decades of combined plant experience. Our work moves VHP off continuous tunnels onto modular batch chambers, and rebuilds the aeration phase, which is normally what decides how long a cycle actually takes.
